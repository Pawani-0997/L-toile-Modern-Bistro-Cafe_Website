Our Heritage

Crafted with Precision,
Served with Artistry

Founded by Executive Chef Laurent Mercier and Master Barista Elena Rostova, L'Étoile harmonizes classic French gastronomy with modern culinary innovation. Every dish is a tribute to local biodynamic farms and sustainable fisheries.

By morning, sunlight pours over artisanal espresso and buttery viennoiseries. By twilight, soft jazz fills the air alongside craft botanical cocktails and dry-aged delicacies.
